# SIH26095 — NIRIKSHAN AI

## DoSJE Centralized Monitoring & Surprise Inspection Platform

> **Monitor. Verify. Act.**

NIRIKSHAN AI is an AI-powered, risk-based monitoring and inspection platform for welfare institutions and NGOs under government schemes.

It brings together **institutional reporting, CCTV telemetry, AI-based anomaly and risk detection, surprise inspections, geo-tagged field verification, digital evidence capture, beneficiary feedback, and government intervention tracking** into a single auditable workflow.

---

## Key Features

- AI-based Risk & Anomaly Detection
- CCTV / Telemetry Monitoring
- Smart Surprise Inspection Allocation
- Geo-fenced Field Verification
- Biometric Inspector Verification
- Geo-tagged Digital Evidence
- Behavioural Analysis
- Beneficiary Feedback
- AI Courtroom Investigation
- Government Intervention Tracking
- Complete Audit Trail

---

## Role-Based Access

| Role | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Government Official | Monitor institutions, review alerts, and take action |
| PMU / Inspection Officer | Conduct inspections and submit verified evidence |
| NGO / Institution | Submit reports and respond to inspections |
| Beneficiary | Provide feedback and verification inputs |
